HereNow is an app built with Android AI Studio that helps users save time by notifying their loved ones with just a click of a button. This app allows users to assign a custom message to a button, and send an instant notification to their partner's phones to notify them about their current situation. For example, I can assign a button called "Gym" and assign "Currently at the Gym" to the above button. Upon pressing the button, it will instantly send the assigned message as a notification to my partner's phone. The rooms are private and secured and the messages can only be accessed by members within a room. Rooms can have private 1-1 connections as well as multiple connections like a "family". This app can also be used as a home screen widget, which allows you to assign your most frequently used buttons and keep them ready on your home screen, saving you time!
